ASTROPHYSICAL & ACOUSTIC BACKGROUND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In 2003, astronomers using NASA's Chandra X-ray Observatory detected acoustic
pressure waves (sound waves) in the hot intracluster gas surrounding the
supermassive black hole at the center of the Perseus galaxy cluster
(Perseus A / NGC 1275). These waves correspond to a musical note 57 octaves below
middle C (a fundamental frequency of B-flat, ~10 million-year period,
roughly 3.2 x 10^-15 Hz).

In this official sonification by the Chandra team and SYSTEM Sounds, these
astrophysical pressure waves were scanned radially (sweeping clockwise from the
center like radar) and re-synthesized into the human audible range by scaling
them up by 57 and 58 octaves (144 quadrillion and 288 quadrillion times higher
than their original pitch).
